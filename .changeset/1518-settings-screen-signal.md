The Settings screen shows less on every row (UX audit SHELL-09, SHELL-23). A row no longer carries a value-type badge, a source badge or a "Default … · 0 per-channel overrides" footer: one muted line appears only when the value in force differs from the default or channels override it. A changed row is marked by a bar at its edge, a switched-off module's group states its read-only reason once, the "Editing for" channel picker sits in the toolbar beside the search, and the hard-coded "system" group badge is gone. Single-line controls sit to the right of the setting's name.

A setting's description is now at most 120 characters (`check:text-budget`), with the long explanations left to each module's documentation page, and it never falls back to another language: in Polish the screen shows the Polish description or none. Every module that declares settings now ships `settingLabels.<code>.name` and `.description` in English and Polish, so the screen no longer shows English names in Polish. Where a manifest declared one of the shortened descriptions, its `description` is the same shorter sentence.
