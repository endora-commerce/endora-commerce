The *General*, *Shop information* and *Storefront* settings groups now have translated headings on the Settings screen (#476). The `settings` bundle gains `settingGroups.general.name`, `settingGroups.shop.name` and `settingGroups.storefront.name` in English and Polish. Before this, a Polish operator saw the manifest's English names.
