**The content of an invoice issued after a return changes** (issue #435). An invoice or proforma issued once a return has been settled, while the order has no invoice yet, now lists only what the buyer kept: each order line is invoiced at its ordered quantity less the quantity of the order's settled `return`-kind cases resolved with a refund or a credit — whether or not the money has been paid out — and a line returned in full is left off. The discount line carries only the kept goods' share: the order's `discountTotal` less the discount those cases carried back with their refunds (as recorded at settlement), left off when nothing remains. Before, every order line and the whole discount were invoiced, so the amount due included goods the buyer had returned. Replacement, repair and complaint cases change no line; the delivery and payment-surcharge lines, the per-rate VAT on the sum of the nets and `paidTotal` (captured − refunded) are unchanged; an invoice for an order refunded in full is still refused with `422 INVOICE_ORDER_REFUNDED` (a proforma is not). With `returns` switched off, every line is invoiced as before. Invoices already issued are not touched.

**A default return refund can differ by one cent from before.** For an order placed under the per-rate VAT method, the default refund of a case is now the drop in the value of the goods kept — what was left paid less VAT per rate on the nets kept after the case, net of the discount the kept part carries — instead of the value of the returned units on their own, so the kept-lines invoice equals what is left paid to the cent. Example: 3 × 10.01 at 23 % (36.94) refunds 12.32 for one unit, not 12.31. All of an order's returns together still refund exactly what was paid, and a cent an earlier case refunded under the previous rule is absorbed by the next case on the same order. Orders placed before 0.108.0 keep their rule. This is not a breaking change.

`ReturnRefundReadPort` (`returnRefundReadPort`, owned by `@endora-commerce/mod-returns`) gains `listReturnedQuantities(orderId)`, returning the new `OrderReturnedQuantity` (`{ orderItemId, quantity, discount }`) from `@endora-commerce/contracts`. The change is additive for a consumer of the port; an implementation of `ReturnRefundReadPort` outside `@endora-commerce/mod-returns` adds the method.
