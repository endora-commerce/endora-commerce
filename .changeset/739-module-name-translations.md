The organization role form, on the storefront and in the admin Roles tab, heads the Addresses, Carts, Orders, Quote Requests and Returns permission groups in the reader's language. The five modules now ship `module.name` in English and Polish ("Adresy", "Koszyki", "Zamówienia", "Zapytania ofertowe", "Zwroty"); before this, Polish pages showed the English manifest name.
