**The merge and personal-organisation provisioning are held to a deployment's field rules** (`specs/193-admin-form-extensions/` D-300.28, D-300.31).

- `organization.merge.start` is a writer of the organization field set. A chosen value that breaks a tightened rule is refused at start with `422 FIELD_RULE_VIOLATION`. A changed field whose `writePermission` the admin lacks is refused with `403 FIELD_WRITE_FORBIDDEN`. In both cases `details[].path` names the field and nothing is recorded, frozen or enqueued. The order-confirmation e-mail union is held like any changed field. A system actor is held to validation only. The start's hook data carries the outcome (`survivor`), computed by the same function the finalize applies.
- `organization.merge.finalize` is a rule-check-only writer (validation only, in the merge runner's system scope), the backstop for a rule deployed mid-merge. Such a refusal is a new cause of *needs attention*, and resume completes the merge once the rule admits the value.
- The merge screen marks a refused value on its choice row with the rule's message, or that the admin may not change the field, and offers *Keep the survivor's value*. A refused e-mail union is shown on a read-only *Order confirmation e-mails (combined)* row with its remedy. The new strings are in `en` and `pl`.
- Every provisioning path runs one Command, `organization.personal.provision`, a rule-check-only `create` writer. It writes `name`, `status`, `vatStatus`, `registeredAddress` and `taxId`, and reports only the derived `name` as chosen, so `writePermission` never refuses the fixed `vat_exempt`. It runs as the caller's child Command in the standalone registration and the federated sign-in, and as a root Command on the repair (`personalOrganizationPort.ensureForCustomerAccount`) and the detach-to-personal. Those two record one names-only entry each.

**Breaking change** for a consumer of `@endora-commerce/mod-organizations/ports`: `PersonalOrganizationProvisionApi.provisionFor(em, input)` is now `provisionFor({ em, run }, input)`. `run` is the caller Command's `ctx.run`, through which the provisioning runs as its child, on its transaction. Old: `await port.provisionFor(em, input)` inside your Command's `run`. New: `await port.provisionFor({ em, run }, input)` with the `em` and `run` of your `CommandContext`. `customer_accounts` is the one caller in this repository. `PersonalOrganizationService` takes the command bus as an optional fifth constructor argument; the repair and the detach refuse without it.
