The free-return window counts calendar days in a new setting, `returns.time_zone` (#905). The setting is an IANA zone name (default `Europe/Warsaw`), can be set per sales channel, and refuses a zone the runtime does not know with `422 VALIDATION_FAILED`. The window now ends at the end of its last day in that zone, and a last day on a Saturday or Sunday moves to the following Monday. It used to end exactly `free_return_days × 24 h` after the anchor, so a case can now be free-return eligible up to a day (or, over a weekend, up to three days) later than before, never earlier. The anchor is the order's completion (`orders.completed_at`, through `OrderReturnContextPort`), no longer its last edit. A new pure module, `domain/withdrawal-period.ts`, derives the consumer's statutory withdrawal period from that anchor: 14 calendar days, the same weekend roll, and a 7-day grace before the withdrawal function closes. An order that has not been completed has not started its period. Nothing reads it yet besides the free-return window; the withdrawal function and its stored in-period flag come in later releases. `isWithinFreeWindow` takes the time zone as a fourth argument.
