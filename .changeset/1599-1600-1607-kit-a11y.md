Accessibility follow-ups from the UX audit (issues #1599, #1600, #1607).

- **Scope picker** (CMS pages, blocks and templates; blog posts and categories): the *Languages* heading now names the group of language checkboxes, so a screen reader announces "Languages, group" when focus enters it. It used to be a label attached to nothing.
- **New order — delivery and billing address**: the *Pick existing* / *Enter new* buttons are a group named *Address source*, and pressing the option already chosen no longer clears a typed address or a picked one.
- **Pricing settings — Show the list price beside the buyer's price**, and the **Private** setting on a new task and on a task's page, are now the standard on/off switch rather than a checkbox announced as a switch. A task's *Private* switch that cannot be turned on (other people are assigned) stays focusable and says why.
- The unused, never-displayed *Quote requests* settings tab component in the Settings module, and its strings, are removed. The quote-request settings are unchanged.
