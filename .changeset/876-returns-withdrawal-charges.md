Issue #876 — a consumer's withdrawal from the whole order refunds the delivery cost and the payment surcharge (Directive 2011/83/EU art. 13(1), Polish Consumer Rights Act art. 32 ust. 1; owner rulings E1–E3 of 2026-11-07). The case that brings back the last unit of a B2C order (its organization `isPersonal`, read through `organizationDetailsPort`) refunds both, besides the goods, when it is a `return` settled with a refund or a credit. A partial case, a complaint, a replacement or repair, and a business customer's return refund neither, and an order with a line not returnable at purchase is never withdrawn in full.

- Each charge is capped at what was paid for it less what earlier cases refunded. The default is the cap when the case was opened within 14 days of the order's completion or within the channel's longer `returns.free_return_days`, and 0 outside it. A channel set to `0` keeps the 14 days.
- The settlement takes `deliveryRefundAmount` / `surchargeRefundAmount`. An omitted one settles at the default recomputed under the order's lock. A present one settles exactly as sent, `0` included, when it is within the cap (art. 13(2), a premium delivery). One above the cap is refused `422 refund_exceeds_paid` with nothing written. This deliberately differs from the line amounts, where an amount equal to a default means the default approved. A replacement or a repair has a cap of 0. The admin form sends only an amount the operator edited. The prefill reports `charges` (on a settled case `cap` equals `approved`), the case detail `chargeRefunds`, and the `return.settled` audit entry `charges: { delivery: { cap, approved }, surcharge: { cap, approved } }`.
- `totalRefundAmount`, the gateway refund, the credit top-up and the corrective invoice's `total` include the charges, and the correction names them (`CorrectiveInvoiceInput.charges`).
- `returnRefundReadPort.listRefundedCharges` reports what settled returns refunded of each charge.
- The admin settlement form shows a *Delivery cost* and a *Payment surcharge* row when there is something to refund (strings in `mod-i18n`, EN and PL).
- `Migration20261108T125405ReturnsCaseChargeRefunds` adds `default_delivery_refund`, `approved_delivery_refund`, `default_surcharge_refund` and `approved_surcharge_refund` to `return_cases`, `0.00` by default, with no backfill.

**Behaviour change on upgrade.** A B2C return that completes a withdrawal now refunds more than the sum of its lines: the settlement form prefills the delivery and the surcharge, and an API client that settles without the two new fields refunds them. Cases settled before the upgrade are not changed and nothing is refunded retroactively (owner ruling E4). To list B2C orders already returned in full whose delivery or surcharge was not refunded, use the query in *A consumer's withdrawal refunds the delivery and the surcharge* in the upgrade guide. The window that decides the default is counted as the withdrawal period is (#905): from the order's completion, in calendar days of `returns.time_zone`, with a last day on a weekend moved to the Monday.
