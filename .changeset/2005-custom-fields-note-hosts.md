**Two new record types** (issue #2005): *Opportunity note* (`opportunity_note`, follows `crm`) and *Project note* (`project_note`, follows `projects`), labelled in English and Polish on the **Custom fields** screen. Neither offers list filters, value carry, buyer input or Page Builder values. While its owner module is off, the type is not offered and its definitions are not changed; nothing is deleted.
