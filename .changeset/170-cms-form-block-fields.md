The Page Builder editor gains the `forms.Form` block's fields (form picker, field look, size, button look, alignment) and its labels in English and Polish, and previews the block's form — a draft, a closed or a missing form with a note on the canvas — through the admin preview of the `forms` module. Nothing changes for pages without the block, or when `forms` is off.
