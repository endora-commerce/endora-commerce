New `EditableGrid` on `@endora-commerce/admin-kit/components` (feature 161, `specs/161-price-book-editor/research.md` R4): an ARIA data grid for spreadsheet-like editing that knows nothing about its cells' meaning. The caller supplies `columns`, `rows`, `getText(rowKey, columnKey)`, optional `getError`, `isReadOnly` and `renderCell` (keep it stable), and receives every change as one group through `onChange(changes, reason)` — `changes` are `{ rowKey, columnKey, text }`, `reason` is `'edit' | 'paste' | 'fill' | 'clear' | 'undo' | 'redo'`. The grid owns the keyboard model (one tab stop, arrows, Home/End, Ctrl+Home/End, PageUp/PageDown, Enter/F2/Escape/Tab, Shift selection), tab-separated copy and paste (a semicolon line too), fill down (Ctrl/⌘+D), clear, undo and redo of change groups, per-cell `aria-invalid` with its reason, `aria-rowcount`/`aria-rowindex` for paged sets (`totalRowCount`, `rowIndexOffset`), and a polite live region. A `ref` (`EditableGridHandle`) applies an outside change as one undoable group (`apply`), forgets the history (`clearHistory`) and reads the selection (`selection`). Types: `EditableGridProps`, `EditableGridColumn`, `EditableGridRow`, `EditableGridCellChange`, `EditableGridChangeReason`, `EditableGridRenderContext`, `EditableGridSelection`, `EditableGridHandle`. Its strings are in the `core` namespace under `editableGrid.*` (shipped by `@endora-commerce/mod-i18n`).
