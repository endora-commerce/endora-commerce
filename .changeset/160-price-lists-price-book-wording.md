The `price_lists` manifest describes price books (feature 160): the module description reads *Price books — base, catalogue, buyer and sale prices per currency, sales channel and audience — and price display modes.*, and the permissions *View price books and display modes* (`price_lists:read`) and *Edit price books, base prices and display modes* (`price_lists:write`), in English and Polish. They named brackets, pricing rules and a rule-based engine the module no longer has.
