The sales-channel editor groups its form into three sections of one card — Identity, Languages and currencies, Storefront — instead of one undivided column (issue #1552). The warning that the storefront's URLs will change now appears only once a change to the languages or to the default-language prefix switch would move them, and is announced when it does; it reads shorter in both languages.
