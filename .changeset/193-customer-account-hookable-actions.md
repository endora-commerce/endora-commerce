**Customer-account writes are hookable** (`specs/193-admin-form-extensions/` FR-110, FR-114, D-300). `@endora-commerce/contracts` exports `CUSTOMER_ACCOUNT_HOOKABLE_ACTIONS` with three refs, each offering `beforeCommit` and `afterCommit`: `create` (`customer_account.create`), `updateProfile` (`customer_account.update_profile`) and `customFieldsUpdate` (`customer.custom_fields.update`), all on the object type `customer_account`. Their data types are new exports too: `CustomerAccountCreateHookData` (`customerAccount`, the uncommitted record), `CustomerAccountUpdateHookData` (`customerAccount` as the after-state, plus the sorted `changedFields` and `changedCustomFields`) and `CustomerAccountOutbox` (`customerAccountId`, `organizationId` and `changedFields`: ids and field names only). The change is additive.

`customer_accounts` lists the three actions in its manifest `hookableActions` and supplies their hook and outbox data, so a module that depends on it can veto one of these writes inside its transaction, or react after it commits, through the outbox. `customer_account.create` is also run as the child Command of a storefront registration, and a hook sees those runs too, with no `extensions`. `customerAccountCommandPort`'s `create`, `updateProfile` and `setCustomFieldValues` forward the caller's `options.extensions` to the hooks; a key that no present hook module claims is refused with `422 COMMAND_EXTENSION_UNCLAIMED` before anything is written. With no hook registered, the three writes behave as before.
