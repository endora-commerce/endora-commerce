The Dictionary screen (`/dictionaries`) is translated throughout (issue #1456): the title, the tab names, every table header and form label, the region and symbol-position options, and every success and error message now come from the module's bundle in English and Polish. The Countries / Currencies / Languages switcher is a real tab list (arrow keys, Home/End, one tab stop). Each search box has an accessible name, the icon-only row buttons are named, and rows are no longer drag-and-drop: one pair of Move up / Move down buttons per breakpoint reorders them, on the Currencies tab too.
