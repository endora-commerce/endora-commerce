The price-book list's filters use the kit's list toolbar (issue #1440). Search, type, status and currency stay in view; sales channel, audience and member are folded behind *More filters*, which counts the folded filters in use and opens by itself when the address carries one. *Clear all filters* ends the filter row and returns focus to the search box.
