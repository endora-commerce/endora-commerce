The Forms and Form entries lists are now standard list pages. Their filters sit in a toolbar row above the results instead of a card header, with *Clear all filters* at the end of the row while a filter is applied. On the entries list the search, the form and the status are always visible, the statuses are one multi-select, and the sales channel and the date range are behind *More filters*, which shows how many of them are in use. The Forms list now remembers the page size you choose. Cards across the forms screens (the builder, its settings and notifications panels, and the entry detail) use the shared card spacing, so the settings and notifications panels are slightly more compact.
